    02 / A bit about me

    Curiosity, with
    an implementation.

    I’m Ilia Gradina, a Senior Data Scientist specialising in Computer Vision at inDrive. My background brings together applied mathematics, software engineering, and machine learning.

    Since joining inDrive in 2021, I’ve built and developed computer vision systems across document understanding, identity verification, vehicle recognition, and visual content moderation.

    Documents & OCR
    Multilingual text recognition, document parsing, and vision-language models.
    Faces & liveness
    Face detection and recognition, image quality assessment, and liveness detection.
    Vehicles & number plates
    Detection and recognition for cars, motorcycles, and commercial vehicles, alongside number-plate recognition.
    Visual moderation
    Detecting inappropriate content and advertising in images.

    I work across the full ML lifecycle: dataset design and annotation, model training and evaluation, reusable libraries, inference optimisation, and production deployment.

    I started in space ballistics and satellite systems software, then moved to data science at Kantar: NLP, marketing mix modelling, and voice and video analytics. That mathematical and engineering foundation carries into my independent work today: causal dynamics, astrodynamics, and scientific tools in Python and Rust.

    I’ve also contributed to Fedora as a package maintainer since 2014. My COPR repositories provide builds of Maxima and FriCAS — computer algebra tools that connect naturally with my interest in mathematical software.
